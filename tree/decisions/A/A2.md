## Decision

The port targets the rete surface Solenoid actually calls. Upstream features Solenoid doesn't use (controls, the control-flow engine, the type-level scope checks) wait until something needs them.

## Why

Solenoid is the only consumer, and the author said porting just the used parts is fine.
