## Decision

The whole of rete's core and rete-engine is ported, controls and the control-flow engine included. The surface Solenoid calls is ported first and is the part that has to be right before Ruggine goes into Solenoid.

## Why

Upstream is small enough that porting all of it costs little more than porting the used part, and the author asked for the whole thing.
