## Decision

Until Solenoid ships on Ruggine, any part of Ruggine can change in one clean step, and Solenoid is updated in the same change. Nothing is kept around for compatibility.

## Why

Solenoid is the only user, so keeping old shapes alive would only slow the port down. Solenoid holds the same rule for itself.
