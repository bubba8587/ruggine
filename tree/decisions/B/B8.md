## Decision

DataflowEngine.reset clears the node and every node downstream of it through the outputs it computes, like upstream, but walks them with a work list and a visited set instead of recursion.

## Why

On a graph without cycles the answer is the same, and with a cycle upstream recurses until the stack overflows, which in wasm aborts the whole module instead of throwing.
