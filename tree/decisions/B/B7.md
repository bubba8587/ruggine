## Decision

The editor keeps an id index beside its node and connection lists, so getNode and getConnection don't scan. Lists keep upstream's insertion order, and a removal keeps the order of what remains.

## Why

Solenoid looks nodes up by id constantly, often inside loops over nodes, so upstream's linear scan turns those loops quadratic for no change in behavior.
